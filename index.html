Q1. In how many different ways can the letters of the word "ORANGE" be arranged so that the vowels never come together?
Solution:
1. Total arrangements of 6 letters = 6! = 720.
2. Vowels (O, A, E) together: Treat as 1 unit + 3 consonants (R, N, G) = 4 units. Arrangements = 4! × 3! = 144.
3. Vowels never together = Total - Together = 720 - 144 = 576.
Q2. In how many different ways can the letters of the word "DESIGN" be arranged such that the vowels never come together?
Solution:
1. Total arrangements of 6 letters = 6! = 720.
2. Vowels (E, I) together: Treat as 1 unit + 4 consonants (D, S, G, N) = 5 units. Arrangements = 5! × 2! = 120 × 2 = 240.
3. Vowels never together = 720 - 240 = 480.
